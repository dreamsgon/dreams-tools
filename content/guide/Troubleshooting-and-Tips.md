# Troubleshooting and tips

**"Conflict" on the save chip.** You (or an AI, or another tab) changed the same thing. The app tries to join the changes; if it can't, reload and redo the last edit. Click the chip to save now.

**A player can't see something.** Check the page or tab's visibility, the item's eye, that the page isn't inside a secret block, and that the player's group has *See the front page and player pages*. Use **What players see** (Relationships) or **Player view** (maps) to check.

**A roll won't roll.** Look under the button for the hint. A formula may need a value (`{value}` with an empty field) or a stat the roll reads isn't filled in. A roll needs at least one die: `[[1999]]` is just a link.

**Discord isn't getting rolls.** Check the channel's webhook (a deleted one shows in red), the *What goes where* choice for the post kind, and press **Post** or set **Always**.

**Google Sheet link fails.** Published links must be on `docs.google.com`, up to 2 MB, and published as CSV for the chosen tab. Google sign-in needs the owner's one-time setup.

**An AI check says "no key".** Use **Copy prompt for my AI app** instead, or add an API key under Connect an AI.

**A map won't load.** Hex maps and world maps are generated in your browser; try a smaller size, then press Reroll.

## Tips

- Keep a **settings code** (`HEX1-…`) or the **seed** with each generated map to recreate it.
- Use a **secret block** for anything you'd hate a player to see by accident.
- Use **tags** and **Ctrl+K** to jump around a big campaign.
- Press **Ctrl/Cmd+S** often; it never hurts.
- Export a **backup** before big changes (Campaigns page and Maptable export).
