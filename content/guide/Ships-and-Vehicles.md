# Ships and vehicles

Choose **Vehicle** as the entry type for a ship, airship, carriage, car or anything the party rides in.

## Overview tab

Pick the **type** (Starship, Sailing ship, Airship, Carriage or Car), write a **model line**, choose an **owner** (a character or organization) and the **captain, coachman or driver** (a character), add a picture or deck plan (otherwise the type's outline shows), and set **where it is** (a place page or a few words). **Gauges** such as Hull, Shields and Fuel use − and +; each type has its own **stats** and **Right now** fields.

Every value has an **eye**: a hidden value never reaches the player site. Players see the Overview only when its tab visibility is Player visible. The page's text is the **Description** tab. Changing a vehicle's type keeps the values whose names match.

## Crew tab

Called **Crew**, **Retinue** (carriage) or **Passengers** (car). It lists the vehicle's **stations**: rename, add or remove them, seat a character page or a typed name, and **pin** a station to show it on the Overview's picture. Below are the **manifest** (everyone else aboard, with roles) and **unnamed counts** such as Marines or Outriders. Every station, person and count has an eye. A character or organization page lists the vehicles it owns, commands or crews in its side panel.

## Hold and Systems tabs

![The Hold tab](images/vehicle-hold.jpg)

- **Hold** (Cargo hold on a starship, Luggage on a carriage): cargo with amounts in the type's unit, against an optional capacity, with a bar that turns red when over.
- **Systems** (Guns and rigging on a sailing ship, Mods on a car): weapons, engines, sails or anything else, each **Working**, **Damaged** or **Offline**, with a note and an optional roll such as `2d6+1`. Click the roll to roll it (players too, when the tab is shared). Rolls can go to Discord.

Both tabs have their own visibility and each line an eye.

## Log tab

The **Log** tab keeps what happened to the vehicle, newest first.

1. Choose a kind: **Note**, **Voyage**, **Battle**, **Repairs** or **Trade**.
2. Add an **In-game date** if you like, and write what happened.
3. Press **Add to log**.

Each entry shows its kind, its in-game date and the real date it was added. Press the eye on an entry to hide it from players or show it again. Press the pencil to change an entry and **Save**, or the bin to remove it. The log keeps the newest 300 entries.

The Log tab has its own visibility, GM only to start. Set it to Player visible to share the log; players then see the entries whose eye is open, with their kind, date and text.

## Vehicle types

The gear beside the type opens **Vehicle types**: make your own kinds for the world (a walker, a dragon-drawn barge, a submarine) or change the ready-made ones: outline, what the commander and Crew tab are called, gauges (bar or pips, maximum, unit, colour), stats, Right now fields, stations and unnamed counts, and the names of the Hold and Systems tabs and the cargo unit. Saving tidies the world's vehicles: values for fields a type no longer has are dropped, and vehicles of a deleted type become the first type.

## Roll up a vehicle

The dice beside the type opens **Roll up a vehicle**. It fills in a vehicle for you from built-in lists of names, stats, cargo and systems.

1. Pick a **Type** and a **template** for its outline, or leave **Any**.
2. Press **Reroll** until the result suits you. You can change the name in the box above the preview.
3. Press **Fill this page** to put the rolled parts on this vehicle, or **Add as new page** to make a second vehicle page beside it.

The templates for each ready-made type are:

| Type | Templates |
|---|---|
| Starship | Freighter, Frigate, Courier, Salvager |
| Sailing ship | Sloop, Brigantine, Galleon |
| Airship | Scout, Cargo hauler, Pleasure barge |
| Carriage | Mail coach, Noble's carriage, Wagon |
| Car | Muscle car, Van, Wasteland rig |

**Fill this page** replaces the model line, stats, gauges, **Right now**, cargo and systems. The crew, owner, location and what players can see stay as they are, and you are asked to confirm first. **Add as new page** makes a GM-only vehicle page. If this page has no real name yet, **Rename this page** is ticked and the rolled name becomes its title. Nothing is saved until you press one of the two buttons.

A vehicle type you made gets the rolled values whose names match its own stats and Right now fields.

## Add to Maptable

When the campaign is paired with a Maptable campaign (see [Getting started](Getting-Started)), the Overview has an **Add to Maptable** button beside the type. It opens Maptable in a new tab and puts the vehicle on the open scene as a large token, with its picture copied in as a character's is.

Starships, sailing ships and airships cover 3×3 squares. Other vehicles cover 2×2, or more when the picture is framed bigger.

## Holdings and character pages

- An organization that owns a vehicle lists it in its first **Holdings** block, marked **vehicle · owner**. The vehicle is not stored in Holdings, so change the owner on the vehicle page. Players see it only when the vehicle's Overview is Player visible and its owner is not GM only.
- A character's side panel lists the vehicles it owns, commands, sits in or crews, under **Vehicles** with the roles, such as Owner, Captain or a seat name. Player character pages show the same **Vehicles** line for shared vehicles only, so a GM-only seat or crew place never shows.

## Character trackers

Characters have small gauges too; see [Characters and the rollable sheet](Characters-and-the-Rollable-Sheet).

> **Tutorial: stat up a sailing ship**
> 1. New entry › **Vehicle** › *Sailing ship*, name it *The Gull*.
> 2. Overview: owner the captain's character page, location *Port Vale*, Hull 40/40.
> 3. Crew: seat the first mate; add a count *Deckhands* of 12.
> 4. Systems: add *Ballista* with roll `2d6+1`, set it to Damaged; players see it only if the tab is shared.
> 5. Log: choose *Voyage*, write *Left Port Vale with the tide*, and press **Add to log**. Set the Log tab to Player visible to share it.
> 6. Press **Add to Maptable** beside the type to put *The Gull* on the open scene as a large token.
