# Relationships and family trees

## Connections: typed ties between pages

Every page except folders and task lists has a **Connections** tab (a quest shows its ties in its own Connections tab).

1. Press **Add a tie**, or drag a page onto the tab from the sidebar.
2. Pick the **kind of tie**. Each reads from both ends, such as *Parent of* / *Child of*, or *Owes a debt to* / *Is owed by*.
3. Optionally add a note, when it began and ended, how each side feels, and whether players see it. A **GM-only** tie can have a **cover story**: another kind of tie that players see instead.

25 built-in kinds come in eight groups (Family, Heart, Conflict, Allegiance, Guidance, Dealings, Belonging, Other). **Kinds of tie** renames built-in kinds and makes your own for anything that joins two pages.

Also listed, without being added: organization members, vehicle owners, seats and crew, places, quests that name the page, and siblings found through shared parents. A tie is stored once on the page it starts from and shows on both. Players get a Connections tab with shared ties and cover stories to pages they can read.

## The Relationships web

**Relationships**, beside the Codex in the sidebar (More on a phone), draws every tie in the campaign as one web.

- Lines are coloured by group and dashed when GM only; pages are coloured by kind and sized by how connected they are.
- **Filter** by group of tie and kind of page. **Members and crew** draws organization members and vehicle crew as dotted lines. **Find a page** focuses one.
- **Click a page** to focus it (its ties, or two steps out) and to **Add a tie**; **click a line** to see and change its ties.
- Drag to move, scroll to zoom. At most the 150 most connected pages are drawn.
- **What players see** shows the web as players would get it: shared ties and cover stories between pages they can read.

## Family trees

On Relationships, switch from **Web** to **Family trees**. A family organization (Noble house, House, Family, Clan, Dynasty) also gets a **Family tree** tab.

![A family tree](images/family-tree.jpg)

- Drawn from family ties (Parent of, Married to, Sibling of, Guardian of, and any kind set to draw on a tree): eldest on top, **couples side by side** with a double line, children below, **wards** on a dashed line, GM-only ties dashed in amber.
- Pick a family (each family organization, then any group of relatives no organization covers) or **Start from a person**.
- A house's tree follows its own line: someone who married in is drawn without their own parents, and their box says which other house they come from. The organization's Profile role (Heir, Abbess) shows on the box.
- **Click a person** to see their family and add a parent, partner, child or sibling, from an existing page or a new character by name.
- On a phone the tree is an outline, eldest first.

## Generators

Both generators use built-in name and trait lists. **Nothing is saved until you add the result.**

### Generate a family

On Family trees: build a family around a character or start a new one. Choose:

- generations above (none, parents, or grandparents too);
- a partner and children, and how many children per couple;
- **twists** (quiet, some or scandalous): a secret child (shown to players as a ward), rival or estranged siblings, an old flame, a betrayal. Most are GM only;
- a name style, whether people who marry in take the family name, and whether to make a Family organization page.

**Reroll** until you like it, then add all characters and ties at once. New characters start GM only.

### Generate ties

On the Web: pick pages (or a few at random, or an organization's members) and an amount of each group of tie from **Off** to **Lots**. It suggests ties that fit, each with a note, skipping pairs already tied. Change each suggestion's kind, note and GM-only setting, keep it through rerolls, or leave it out.

![Generate ties](images/generate-ties.jpg)

### Write with AI

With an API-key AI connection both generators offer **Write with AI**. Add an optional steer (tone, theme, hook) and it rewrites the notes from what the pages say and, for a family, writes a line about each new person that becomes their page. It reads only what that connection may see; a players-only connection leaves out GM-only pages and ties. Nothing is saved until you add the result, and tokens count against the connection's monthly limit.

> **Tutorial: a noble house in a minute**
> 1. Relationships › **Family trees** › **Generate a family** › *Start a new person*.
> 2. Grandparents, partner and children, 3 children, *Some* twists, make a Family organization.
> 3. Reroll twice, then **Add** everything.
> 4. Click the eldest child, **Add partner**, then press **What players see** to check your secrets stay secret.
