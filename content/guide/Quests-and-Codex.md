# Quests and the Codex

## Quests

A **Quest** is a page kind with a **state** (rumour, active, done, failed) and a **category** (Main, Side and Personal to start; each campaign can make its own with a name and colour from the **Categories** button on the quest board).

### The quest board

A **Quests** view per campaign shows a **board** you drag cards across, and a journal-style list. A quest page shows the same controls at the top.

### The quest page

A quest has tabs **Overview**, **Log**, **GM**, **Connections** and **Journal** (the briefing players read).

![A quest's Connections tab](images/quest-connections.jpg)

**Overview**

- **Given by**, **Reward** and **Where** appear as cards. Pick each from a search (a bottom sheet on a phone) or drag a page from the sidebar. The giver can be any page: a character, an organization, a place.
- The **Reward** holds words and up to 20 pages (an item, a deed) and has its own eye. Hidden rewards are not shown to players, who see "Reward: not known yet".
- **Goals** read as sentences: choose a verb (Find, Rescue, Recover, Deliver, Investigate, Defeat, Escort, Reach, Protect, Persuade, Learn, Meet, or your own word), a target (a campaign page or typed name) and an optional second part ("last seen at", "to", "from"), for example *Find Arin Vale, last seen at the Old Mill*. Each goal has a status (To do, Done, Failed, Dropped), its own eye for players, a note, and Move up/down/Remove.

**Log**

Reads like a conversation, oldest at top, each entry signed. The GM writes with a **session label** (suggested from your sessions), chooses **Players see this** or **GM only**, and can change visibility or delete from an entry's menu. **Ctrl/Cmd+Enter** sends. On the player site a signed-in member can add to the Log of a quest they can read and edit or delete only their own entries.

**GM** holds private preparation that never reaches players.

**Connections** lists every page the quest touches, worked out from the quest itself (giver, each goal's target and place, where it happens, the reward pages, links in its text, log entries) and from ties you add. It is available as a list or a web (see [Relationships and family trees](Relationships-and-Family-Trees)).

> **Tutorial: a quest in one minute**
> 1. New entry › **Quest** › *The Missing Miller*.
> 2. **Given by**: drag the village elder from the sidebar. **Where**: the Old Mill.
> 3. Add a goal: **Find** › *Arin Vale* › "last seen at" › *Old Mill*.
> 4. Log: write *Session 4: The party found the mill burned* and leave it on *Players see this*.

## Codex

Any page can join a campaign's **Codex** under a category (**Bestiary**, **Items & relics**, **Lore & legends**, **Letters & notes**, **Places**, or your own). Each campaign records what its players **found** and when. Players get a **Codex** of their finds, showing the picture and a player summary, never the page text or unfound entries.

An **inbox** holds quick notes from the table, to be filed later as an entry or into a quest's log. Creature entries are characters, so they can become Maptable tokens.
