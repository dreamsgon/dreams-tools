# Limits and what's coming

## Good to know

- **Accounts and sharing**: separate accounts; no live multiplayer yet. Players join by link or through Members.
- **Storage**: the Encyclopedia workspace is limited to 2 MB; atlas images up to 8 MB each. Uploaded map icons up to 1 MB, up to 40 per map.
- **Maptable and Encyclopedia stay separate**: pairing links them but maps do not sync automatically.
- **Maptable fog**: manual fog; automatic wall-based vision is not built.
- **Offline mode** is not available.
- **Email**: signup, confirmation and reset email for ordinary users needs custom SMTP set up by the site owner.
- **Some integrations are unverified in the real world**: Google sign-in (needs the owner's Google project), writing HP back to a sheet, the Foundry module in a real world, and real-key AI checks (tested with mocked answers).

## Planned or in progress

- **Space and planets**: share the Galaxy, Hex map and Survey chart tabs with players (charted systems only).
- **Hex maps**: painting hexes, regenerating while keeping painted hexes, and revealing hexes around tokens.
- **Stores**: a store as a Maptable token, calendar hours, restocking from a random table, live trading between players.
- **Vehicles**: a New vehicle generator, a Log tab, and Add to Maptable as a large token; vehicle pages from the AI's `create_page`.
- **Bridges**: more kinds in the Bridge API and Foundry module (journals, quests, scenes, tokens, tables, calendar, combat); D&D Beyond link import (unofficial); Roll20 and Fantasy Grounds.
- **Imported sheets**: HP − / + on the page and from tokens.
- **Sheet formulas**: field references (so a skill can follow its ability).
- **Members**: the campaign forum.
- **AI**: a "Show earlier messages" control for long chats; reading private Drive files for recaps.
- **Dungeons**: more generator controls (the Map Creator's Generate already makes dungeons, caves, villages and wilderness).
