# Limits and what's coming

## Good to know

- **Accounts and sharing**: separate accounts; no live multiplayer yet. Players join by link or through Members.
- **Storage**: each campaign in the Wiki, and each world’s shared lore, is stored on its own and limited to 2 MB, which you can raise to 5, 10 or 20 MB in the Wiki’s Settings, Storage and holds up to 5,000 pages. All live campaigns together start at 60 MB, which you can raise to 100 or 150 MB in the same place. Archiving a finished campaign takes its pages out of the Wiki so they count toward nothing; atlas images up to 8 MB each. A Maptable scene holds up to 2,000 tokens, and archived maps don't load with the rest. Random tables: up to 10,000 tables and 10 MB per account. Uploaded map icons up to 1 MB, up to 40 per map.
- **Maptable and Wiki stay separate**: pairing links them but maps do not sync automatically.
- **Maptable fog**: manual fog; automatic wall-based vision is not built.
- **Offline mode** is not available.
- **Your own entry types**: the AI connection and the VTT bridges can make Page and Other pages, but not pages of a type you made.
- **Email**: signup, confirmation and reset email for ordinary users needs custom SMTP set up by the site owner.
- **Some integrations are unverified in the real world**: Google sign-in (needs the owner's Google project), writing HP back to a sheet, the Foundry module in a real world, and real-key AI checks (tested with mocked answers).

## Planned or in progress

- **Space and planets**: share the Galaxy, Hex map and Survey chart tabs with players (charted systems only).
- **Hex maps**: clearing fog around the party when an AI or a player moves a token (today only the GM's own moves in Maptable do), and a player-view fog preview in the generator.
- **Stores**: a store as a Maptable token, calendar hours, restocking from a random table, live trading between players.
- **Vehicles**: the vehicle Hold as a container for store-style taking and putting.
- **Bridges**: more kinds in the Bridge API and Foundry module (scenes, tokens, tables, calendar, combat; quest changes made in Foundry); D&D Beyond link import (unofficial); filling the D&D 5E by Roll20 sheet's NPC section from the Roll20 script; Fantasy Grounds rulesets other than 5E, and filling a Fantasy Grounds player's own character sheet.
- **Imported sheets**: HP − / + on the page and from tokens.
- **Sheet formulas**: field references (so a skill can follow its ability).
- **Members**: the campaign forum.
- **AI**: a "Show earlier messages" control for long chats; reading private Drive files for recaps.
