# Getting started

## What you need

An email and password account. Sign up on the RPG Tools home page and confirm your email. Each account has its **own** Maptable maps, Wiki worlds, uploads, tags and AI connections. Accounts are not merged and there is no shared, real-time multiplayer yet; players join through links and the Members system (see [Campaigns, players and sharing](Campaigns-Players-and-Sharing)).

## The two tools

| Tool | Address | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Maptable | `/maptable` | Running the table: maps, tokens, fog, initiative, dice. Opens on the Scene Picker of the campaign you were last in |
| Wiki | `/encyclopedia` | Prepping and remembering: pages, characters, quests, maps, calendars |
| Campaigns | `/campaigns` | One list of every campaign in either tool |

A shared header at the top (the RPG Tools name, **Maptable** and **Wiki** tabs, and your account) is on every page. The two tools stay separate: a map in one is not automatically copied into the other. You can *link* them, as described next.

## Campaigns across both tools

![The Campaigns page](images/campaigns.jpg)

Open **Campaigns**. It lists Maptable campaigns and Wiki campaigns together.

- **Pair them.** A Wiki campaign can be paired with a Maptable campaign. Pairing moves and copies nothing, and unpairing leaves both as they were. **Match your campaigns** suggests pairs with the same name.
- **New campaign** creates it in either tool, or both. **Add maps** or **Start notes** fills in the missing half later. Renaming renames both.
- A private backup of both workspaces is kept before the first change, and the page offers a backup download.
- **Folders.** Campaigns can sit in folders, which can be nested. Drag a campaign onto a folder, or use the **Folder** choice in its Details panel. Removing a folder moves its campaigns up a level and deletes nothing.

> **Tutorial: set up your first campaign (5 minutes)**
> 1. Sign in and open **Campaigns**.
> 2. Choose **New campaign**, name it, and create it in both tools.
> 3. Open its card to reach the **campaign hub** (`/campaigns/<campaign>`): a Dashboard (scenes, next session, recent pages, party) and the Front page.
> 4. Use **Open Wiki** and **Open in Maptable** for that campaign's table (the scene you last had open in it, else its first scene). Every link on the hub, including the Maptable and Wiki links at the top of the site, opens this campaign.

## Finding your way around

![Quick search with Ctrl+K](images/quick-search.jpg)

- **Breadcrumbs** show where you are. Every level is a button, the small arrow beside it lists other places at that level, and **Alt+Up** goes up a level. On a phone you get a Back button and a title that opens the whole path.
- **Search: Ctrl/Cmd+K** opens quick search in the Wiki. Jump to a page, create one with the name you typed, or go to the Dashboard, Characters, Maps, Calendar or Maptable. Type `#tagname` to list everything with a tag.
- **Stay put.** A refresh or the Back button returns you to the same page, scene or tab. Both tools remember their last place per browser.
- **Folders** everywhere (scenes, tokens, assets, Wiki sidebar): click anywhere on a folder row to open or close it. Rename and move sit behind the pencil. Re-order by dragging onto a folder's top or bottom edge, with Move up / Move down, or with **Alt+Up / Alt+Down**.

## Saving

Both tools show a **save chip** in the header: Saved, Unsaved changes, Saving…, Not saved · Retry, or Conflict. Click it to save now, or press **Ctrl/Cmd+S**. Work also autosaves. If you edit on two devices at once, the app joins non-clashing edits and shows a Conflict notice only when it must.

## Appearance

The **Appearance** menu is per device:

- Theme: Light, Dark or System; six accent colours.
- Fonts for interface, titles and reading (Google Fonts, loaded only when you choose one).
- **Text size**: 85%, 92%, 100%, 110% or 125%.
- **Page width**: Narrow, Standard, Wide or Full.
- Reduce motion, and Reset.

## Tags

Make your own coloured tags with **+ Tag** on Wiki pages and characters, on campaigns, and in Maptable scene settings. Each tool keeps its own tag list. The **Tags manager** renames, recolours, merges and deletes. Click a tag, or type `#name` in search, to see everything with it.

## Moving in from elsewhere

- Export a JSON backup from the old app (with embedded images) and restore it after signing in.
- For the Wiki, import from its Settings; imports make separate copies and never overwrite. Uploaded atlas images come as separate **Map packages**.
- Player links and AI keys do not migrate; make new ones.
